# Storyboard — Stride Circle 20s product promo

**Format:** 1920×1080 landscape  
**Audio:** silent / music-ready visual pass; no external music asset in this version  
**Style basis:** DESIGN.md + saved app screens in `../../assets/screenshots/`

## Global direction

Create a light, social walking promo with the product UI as proof. Each beat uses a confident editorial headline on the left and a large phone screen on the right. Orbit lines, dots, and soft card depth turn the app’s progress language into a visual walking path. The close shifts to navy so the blue icon and “Move together” promise land with more weight.

## Beat 1 — Hook / 0.0–4.1s

- **Message:** Move together.
- **Visual:** Home screen in a dark rounded phone frame; orbit rings behind it; two floating proof cards.
- **Motion:** Kicker slides in, headline springs up, phone settles with a soft rotation, screen receives a slow zoom, proof cards arrive on different eases, lower card gently bobs.
- **Transition:** Blue full-frame wipe at 4.0s.
- **Assets:** `../../assets/screenshots/home.png`.

## Beat 2 — Personal proof / 4.1–9.0s

- **Message:** Your progress, in view.
- **Visual:** History screen with two pale-sky proof pills for best day and seven-day story.
- **Motion:** Copy staggers from the left; phone enters with a slight counter-rotation; the history screen drifts upward with a subtle scale.
- **Transition:** Peach full-frame wipe at 9.0s.
- **Assets:** `../../assets/screenshots/history.png`.

## Beat 3 — Social payoff / 9.1–14.0s

- **Message:** Find your circle.
- **Visual:** Circles screen and a blue “Hi Walkers” badge that reads like an invitation.
- **Motion:** Phone enters from the right with a slight counter-rotation; badge springs in then floats; dots keep the scene alive.
- **Transition:** Yellow full-frame wipe at 14.0s.
- **Assets:** `../../assets/screenshots/circles.png`.

## Beat 4 — Product close / 14.1–20.0s

- **Message:** Make every walk count.
- **Visual:** Navy close with icon/wordmark lockup, a single CTA, and the Circle Race screen as tangible product evidence.
- **Motion:** Headline and lockup rise in; phone settles from the right; CTA breathes with a gentle pulse; the orbit field stays quiet and premium.
- **Transition:** None; let the final lockup hold through the end.
- **Assets:** `../../assets/icon.png`, `../../assets/screenshots/circle-race.png`.
