# Stride Circle Promo Design System

## Overview

Stride Circle is a warm, calm walking app with a light canvas, expressive navy typography, and bright action blue used to make progress feel tangible. The product UI uses rounded cards, generous whitespace, and small peach/yellow moments of encouragement. The promo carries that same approachable energy into a clean editorial layout: large copy on the left, phone UI evidence on the right, and orbiting path lines as a visual metaphor for shared movement.

## Colors

- **Warm canvas**: `#F9FBFF` — primary background.
- **Navy ink**: `#111827` — headings, device frame, and high-contrast text.
- **Action blue**: `#2F66E8` — progress, active states, and the main product accent.
- **Soft sky**: `#EAF2FF` — progress panels and calm UI surfaces.
- **Sky strong**: `#CFE1FF` — orbit lines and secondary decoration.
- **Peach**: `#FF9A67` — warm encouragement accent.
- **Yellow**: `#F7C84B` — milestone accent for transitions.

## Typography

- **Primary**: Nunito Sans, 600–900. Rounded, friendly, and used across all promo copy.
- **Display**: Nunito Sans 900, tight tracking. Headlines are large and compact for a confident launch tone.
- **Labels**: Nunito Sans 800 with generous tracking and uppercase treatment.

## Elevation

The app is mostly flat with thin borders and soft depth rather than hard shadows. The promo uses the same rule: phone frames get diffuse shadows, floating proof cards use translucent white and blur, and orbit lines create depth without adding heavy decoration.

## Components

- **Phone evidence frame**: Rounded dark device shell with a clipped product screenshot and soft shadow.
- **Progress proof pill**: Pale-sky rounded cards with a large value and small uppercase label.
- **Orbit path field**: Circular and dashed arcs that make the walking journey feel connected.
- **Encouragement card**: Translucent white card with a warm peach or blue proof value.
- **Action lockup**: Product icon, wordmark, and a single blue CTA for the close.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Keep the canvas bright and breathable.
- Let the real product screens do the proving.
- Use blue for action and progress, peach/yellow for small emotional accents.
- Give each beat one dominant headline and one dominant UI surface.
- Use smooth, friendly spring motion with visible but not frantic movement.

### Don'ts

- Do not turn the product into a hard-edged competitive fitness ad.
- Do not use dark backgrounds except for the final lockup.
- Do not add invented health claims, rewards, or performance guarantees.
- Do not bury the phone screens under effects or dense copy.
- Do not use sharp, glitchy, or aggressive transitions.
