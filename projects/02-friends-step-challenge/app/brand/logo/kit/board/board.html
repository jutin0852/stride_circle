What we heard

The brief

A social walking app where small circles of friends compare daily steps, keep streaks and cheer each other on. It should feel encouraging, playful and everyday, not like a hardcore fitness tracker.

socialencouragingplayfuleveryday

Success: avoids fitness cliches (activity rings, running figures, swooshes), says circle and steps together, works as an app icon and in one colour.

Final design

The walking loop

Eight left-right steps walking one perfect circle; the blue step is today's step.

  • Says circle and steps in one shape, with no running man or activity ring
  • The blue lead step works as a live accent: progress, streaks and notifications
  • Small-size cut (six steps in a blue disc) keeps it readable at 16 to 48 px
