Why B: Your steps and your circle share one space. The connection feels immediate, while the illustration stays small enough to leave room for friends.

Interaction & design notes

Try Sync, the circle switcher, and Nice work. Circles, History and Profile are clearly labeled preview placeholders. All totals and people are fictional sample data; no health connection is made.

The pebble companion is an original exploratory character, not a finalized mascot. White space, saturated accents and tactile bottom edges borrow the reference’s visual principles, not its artwork.

Goal feedback follows a simulated confirmed sync. Optional native behavior: one light haptic and a short, opt-in chime. Neither is implemented here. Reduced motion uses the same confirmation text with a static joyful pose. No loops, penalties or shame for missed days.

Sample competition timezone: Africa/Lagos, WAT (UTC+1). Goal state: 8,000 steps, second today, existing 12-day streak acknowledged. These assumptions are for review.

Designed in source for 393px and narrow 320px layouts. Post-generation rendering and automated checks were not performed under this workflow.
